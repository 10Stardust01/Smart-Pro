# SMART-PRO department review checklist

Use the department's approved test accounts and sample records for the walkthrough.

- [ ] **HOD and course coordinator.** Check that each offering has the correct
  coordinator, review stages, marking rules, submission deadlines and result
  publishers. Confirm that each coordinator sees the offerings assigned to them.
- [ ] **HOD and Registrar.** Record who can approve and enter a faculty appointment.
  Decide who can change its end date, end it early or reinstate it, and what
  approval must be recorded. Confirm how the department will handle an urgent
  access removal while retaining marks already submitted.
- [ ] **HOD and Admin.** Review the Admin replacement and ordinary HOD handover
  using test accounts. Name the institutional approver for exceptional succession
  if the HOD cannot resign, and the authorized operator for the first HOD.
- [ ] **Registrar.** Confirm which student and faculty exports are official, what
  evidence matches a record to an account, and when downloaded Google Forms
  responses may be used. Review conflicting records before they affect a profile.
- [ ] **Admin and a faculty member.** Walk through granting faculty access, changing
  a project guide and ending that guide assignment. Admin also appoints offering
  coordinators. Confirm the faculty member
  loses access to the former project while the completed academic history stays
  available to authorized staff.
- [ ] **Student, faculty member and authorized publisher.** Have the student
  submit a sample PDF, the faculty member record feedback and the publisher
  release the result. Review an approved correction. Check that the student
  sees their own result and that authorized staff can still see the original version.
- [ ] **Accessibility and records leads.** Try the key tasks with a keyboard and a
  screen reader. Agree which document formats are acceptable and who will
  provide an accessible copy when needed. Confirm who may see student records
  and how long records and backups are kept.
- [ ] **IT and operations leads.** Name the owner of sign-in, account support,
  hosting, backups and recovery. Confirm who receives reminders and handles
  delivery failures. Ask the recovery owner to demonstrate that the selected
  deployment can restore both records and documents.
- [ ] **Department sign-off.** Record any failed checks and their owners. Approve
  real-user use only after IT and the department sign off on the outstanding
  access, privacy, accessibility and recovery checks.
